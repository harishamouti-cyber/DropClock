**DropClock solves this instantly.** By displaying dynamic, real-time fulfillment cutoff countdowns and accurate delivery dates right next to your "Add to Cart" button, you give customers the clarity, trust, and urgency they need to check out immediately.

#### 1. High-Converting Product Page Countdown Pills
- **Real-Time Dispatch Countdown:** Displays live hours and minutes remaining until today's dispatch cutoff (e.g., *"Order within 3 hrs 42 mins · Dispatched Today"*).
- **Dynamic Delivery ETAs:** Automatically calculates calendar arrival dates based on working days and shipping transit lead times (e.g., *"Delivered by Wednesday, Oct 7"*).
- **Multiple Visual Styles:** Choose from **Capsule Pill**, **Minimal Line**, or **Urgency Bar** presets to match your storefront typography and brand palette seamlessly.

#### 3. Post-Purchase Order Status & Thank You Timeline
- **3-Step SLA Confidence Tracker:** Replaces ambiguous shipping updates with an interactive timeline: *Order Placed* $\rightarrow$ *Dispatched Today* $\rightarrow$ *Estimated Arrival*.

#### 4. Granular Warehouse & Fulfillment Logic
- **Store Timezone & Cutoff Hour:** Align countdown timers with your actual warehouse dispatch schedule.
- **Operating Days & Blackout Dates:** Skip non-working weekends and scheduled warehouse holidays with zero arithmetic errors.
- **Product Tag Overrides:** Assign custom lead times for specific items (e.g., apply a 14-day delivery window to `pre-order` or `freight` items).

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Will DropClock slow down my store or impact my Google Lighthouse score?
**No.** DropClock is engineered to be featherweight. The entire storefront bundle is under 5KB of pure, vanilla JavaScript without bloated libraries or third-party tracking scripts. It loads asynchronously via Shopify's native Content Delivery Network (CDN) and executes with zero Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).

### 2. What happens to my theme if I uninstall DropClock?
**Your theme remains 100% clean.** DropClock is built strictly as a Shopify Theme App Extension. It does not modify `theme.liquid` or paste snippet files into your template directories. When you deactivate or uninstall the app, every trace is immediately and cleanly removed by Shopify.

### 3. Can I set different delivery lead times for pre-orders or oversized freight items?
**Yes.** In DropClock Studio, you can configure granular Product Tag rules. For example, tagging a product with `pre-order` or `heavy-freight` will automatically calculate arrival dates using your custom lead time (e.g., 14 days) instead of your standard express dispatch window.

### 4. Does DropClock support multi-currency and international stores?
**Yes.** DropClock automatically respects your active storefront currency format and symbol. All customer-facing text strings (countdown labels, delivery badges, and progress bar messages) are fully customizable in DropClock Studio to support local languages.
